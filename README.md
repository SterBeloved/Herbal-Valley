# 🌿 Herbal Valley 🍵
### A Little Journey Through a Valley of Healing Herbs

*Somewhere between the mountains and the moonlight, a little herbal adventure awaits...* ✨

Welcome to **Herbal Valley**, a cozy, interactive browser game inspired by traditional Chinese herbal culture, paper-cut illustrations, and the quiet magic of nature.

Wander through a whimsical valley, discover medicinal plants, collect herbs, and help a friendly old Master prepare a special herbal brew.

No rush. Just a little curiosity, a little patience, and a whole valley waiting to be explored. 🌼

---

## 🌷 What Awaits You?

### 🏡 Explore a Paper-Cut Valley
Travel through six little herbal regions, each filled with plants waiting to be discovered.

Watch the valley transform between **day and night**, accompanied by gentle forest ambience.

### 🌱 Meet 16 Chinese Herbs
Discover traditional Chinese herbs through interactive exploration.

Collect your favourites in a little basket and learn more about them in the **Field Guide**. 📖

### 🫖 Become the Master's Apprentice
Step inside the Master's Apothecary and help prepare a herbal brew!

Follow the Master's instructions as you:

1. 🪵 Find your tools
2. 🌿 Select and prepare your herbs
3. 🥣 Grind them with a mortar and pestle
4. 💧 Add water and prepare the clay pot
5. 🔥 Light the stove
6. 🥄 Stir gently and wait for the brew
7. 🍵 Present your creation to the Master

But be careful! The Master might have something to say if you pick the wrong tool... 👀

### 🌙 Discover a Moonlit Secret
Some stories only reveal themselves after sunset.

Explore the valley at night, follow the hidden clues, and uncover **The Master's Lost Notes**.

Perhaps there's another recipe waiting to be remembered... ✨

### 📜 Keep a Little Herbal Journal
Open the illustrated Field Guide to discover herbs and their traditional cultural associations.

A tiny collection of botanical knowledge, made for curious explorers.

## 🎨 Made with a Little Love

Herbal Valley combines:

- 🎐 Vintage-inspired paper-cut illustrations
- 🌿 Traditional Chinese herbal culture
- 🖱️ Point-and-click exploration
- 📖 Interactive botanical learning
- 🌗 Day and night environments
- 🎵 Gentle forest ambience
- 🍵 A step-by-step herbal brewing mini-game
- ✨ Hidden discoveries and playful interactions

The game is built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, and runs directly in the browser.

---

## 🌸 A Note from the Creator

Herbal Valley began as a small experiment in bringing traditional cultural knowledge into an interactive digital world.

I wanted learning about Chinese herbs to feel less like memorising information and more like opening a little storybook, wandering through a garden, and discovering something for yourself.

This is an evolving personal project, and I hope to keep adding new details, interactions, and little surprises along the way.

Thank you for visiting my little valley! ♡

## 🍃 A Gentle Reminder

Herbal Valley is an educational and creative experience inspired by traditional herbal culture.

The fictional cases and brewing activities are **not medical advice or instructions for real-world treatment**.
